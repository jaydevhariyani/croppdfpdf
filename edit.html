Online PDF Editor (Type & Whiteout)

Make quick corrections to your PDF documents without needing expensive software. Our free online PDF Editor allows you to easily cover up mistakes using the Whiteout tool and type new text directly over your document.

How to Edit a PDF

  1. Click "Choose file" to upload your PDF.
  2. To erase text, click the Whiteout button, then click and drag over the mistake on the document to cover it with a white box.
  3. To add new text, click the Add Text button, click anywhere on the page, and start typing.
  4. Click "Save & Download PDF" to permanently apply your changes and download the updated file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change the existing original text directly?
PDFs are fixed documents. The best and safest way to edit them is to use the Whiteout tool to hide the old text, and then use the Add Text tool to type your correction on top.

Are my documents secure?
Yes. All whiteout and text additions happen entirely in your web browser. Your private documents are never uploaded or saved to our servers.
