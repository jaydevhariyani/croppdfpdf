Online PDF Rotator

Have you ever scanned a document only to realize it was saved upside down or sideways? Our free online PDF Rotator fixes this issue instantly. Permanently rotate PDF pages by 90 degrees to the left or right, or flip them 180 degrees to correct upside-down scans.

How to Rotate a PDF Document

  1. Click "Choose file" and select the misaligned PDF from your computer or phone.
  2. Select the rotation angle you need: Rotate Right (Clockwise 90°), Rotate Left (Counter-clockwise 90°), or Upside Down (180°).
  3. Click the "Rotate PDF!" button. The orientation will be permanently fixed and your new file will download immediately.

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. While most standard PDF viewers (like Adobe Reader or Chrome) have a "View > Rotate" option, that only rotates the file temporarily for reading. The next time you open the file, it will be sideways again. Our tool rotates the actual underlying data, ensuring the document is permanently fixed and prints correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?
Not at all. We change the metadata orientation tags of the document without touching the images or text inside. 100% of the original quality is preserved.

Yes, our servers delete all uploaded and processed files automatically after one hour. Nobody has access to your private documents.
