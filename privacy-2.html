Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Our Promise

CropPDFPDF is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, upload, store or share the PDF files you process. All cropping happens entirely inside your own web browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not upload your PDF files to any server.
  • We do not store your documents or cropped results.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the tool.
  • We do not track the content of the files you open.

2. Information That May Be Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics services (if enabled) may collect standard technical data such as:

  • IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and approximate time spent
  • Referring website

This information is used only to improve site performance, security and user experience. It is never linked to the content of any PDF you process.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Services

We may use cookies or similar technologies for:

  • Essential site functionality
  • Analytics (to understand how visitors use the site)
  • Advertising (Google AdSense or similar compliant networks)

Google AdSense uses cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization through Google’s Ad Settings.

We only work with advertising partners that follow Google’s AdSense program policies and applicable privacy laws.

4. How Your PDFs Are Processed

When you upload a PDF:

  • The file stays in your browser’s memory.
  • Rendering and cropping are performed using client-side JavaScript libraries (PDF.js and PDF-Lib).
  • No copy of the file is sent to our servers or any third party.
  • When you close the tab or click Reset, the data is cleared from memory.

5. Children’s Privacy

CropPDFPDF is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will always reflect the latest version. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
