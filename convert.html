How to Convert a PDF

  1. Click "Choose file" to upload the PDF you want to convert.
  2. Select your desired output format from the dropdown list (e.g., JPG, PNG, DOCX).
  3. If converting to an image, choose the desired resolution (DPI). We recommend 300 DPI for high-quality printing.
  4. Click the "Convert PDF!" button. Your converted files will be generated and downloaded as a ZIP archive if there are multiple pages.

Why convert PDF to JPG or PNG?

  • Social Media Sharing: Most social media platforms and messaging apps prefer image files over PDFs.
  • Web Embedding: Images load faster and display natively on websites without needing a PDF viewer.
  • E-commerce Needs: Some seller platforms require shipping labels to be uploaded specifically as image files rather than PDFs.
