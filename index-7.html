Online PDF Crop Tool

CropPDFPDF is a free browser-based PDF cropper. Select the exact area you want to keep, automatically remove white margins, or add extra space around the page for annotations and printing.

Shipping Label Cropping

Use this tool to crop shipping labels from Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, AJIO, Amazon, LimeRoad and Nykaa. Upload the label PDF, drag the selection box to cover only the label area, then download a clean, print-ready label. Always check the final size and barcode before printing.

Crop options

  • Crop to Selection — drag and resize the blue box to keep only the area you need.
  • Auto-Crop — automatically detects and removes visible white margins.
  • Extend Margins — adds extra whitespace around every page (useful for binding or notes).

How to crop a PDF

  1. Upload one or more PDF files (or drag & drop).
  2. Preview the first page and adjust the crop box by dragging the corners or moving the box.
  3. Choose Crop to Selection, Auto-Crop, or Extend Margins.
  4. Click Crop PDF and download the result.

Privacy

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to our servers. This makes CropPDFPDF suitable for personal and business documents that must stay private.

Does the crop apply to all pages? Yes. The same crop region is applied to every page. If you need different crops, split the PDF first, crop each part, then merge.
Can I crop Flipkart / Meesho / Amazon shipping labels? Yes. Upload the label PDF, visually select only the label area, and download a correctly sized label ready for thermal or regular printers.
Is this tool free? Yes. CropPDFPDF is completely free to use. No registration, no watermarks.
Do you store my files? No. Files stay in your browser memory only for the duration of the session and are discarded when you close the page.
