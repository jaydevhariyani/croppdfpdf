How to Split a PDF File

  1. Click on the "Choose file" button to upload your PDF document.
  2. Once uploaded, choose how you want to split the file. You can enter specific page numbers (e.g., 2, 4, 7) or a range of pages (e.g., 5-10) that you wish to extract.
  3. Click the "Split PDF file!" button. Your new, separated PDF will be generated instantly for download.
