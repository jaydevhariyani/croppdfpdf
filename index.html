What is PDF cropping and why it matters for Indian e-commerce?

PDF cropping changes the visible page area by setting CropBox. Example: courier gives you A4 PDF with 4 labels (2x2). Your TSC, Arks, TVS thermal printer needs one label per 4x6 roll. Cropping selects one label quadrant to create print-ready 4x6 PDF. Scanned docs with black borders also need cropping. For Amazon FBA, Flipkart, Meesho, Shiprocket, correct 10x15cm size is critical - if you print A4 on 4x6, barcode cuts and shipment goes RTO costing ₹100-200 per order. Our tool crops precisely to 101.6x152.4mm, accepted by Delhivery, Xpressbees, Ekart, Blue Dart, DTDC, Shadowfax. Batch crop: same box to all pages.
