Online PDF Crop Tool

CropPDFPDF is a browser-based PDF cropper designed for quick document editing. Select the exact area you want to keep, automatically trim visible whitespace, or extend page margins for annotations and printing.

Shipping Label Cropping

Use the visual cropper for e-commerce shipping labels from Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, AJIO, Amazon, LimeRoad, Nykaa and Tata CLiQ. Always verify the final label dimensions and barcode before printing.

How it works

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Preview the first page and select the area to keep.
  3. Choose Crop to selection, Auto-crop, or Extend margins.
  4. Process and download the new PDF.
