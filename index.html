How to Crop a PDF

  1. Upload your document. If you wish to upload multiple files simultaneously, please ensure that they are of the same size and margins.
  2. If you want to select the part of the document for cropping manually, mark it in the preview window and then choose the Crop to selection option.
  3. For faster processing of shipping labels, use our one-click Auto-Crop buttons designed specifically for popular marketplaces.
  4. It is important to note that all pages in the processed documents will be cropped equally. If you require different parts of each page to be cropped, we recommend splitting the file before manually cropping each page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop a PDF online?
Upload your PDF, then either drag to select the area you want to keep. Click the crop button to process.

Does cropping apply to all pages in the PDF?
Yes. The same crop settings are applied to all pages. If you need different crops for different pages, split the PDF first, crop individual pages, then merge them back together.

Are my documents safe when I use this tool?
Absolutely. Your files are processed entirely within your web browser. We do not store your documents on our servers, ensuring your sensitive data remains private.
