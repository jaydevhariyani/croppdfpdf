How to Crop a PDF

  1. Upload your document. If you wish to upload multiple files simultaneously, please ensure that they are of the same size and margins.
  2. If you want to select the part of the document for cropping manually, mark it in the preview window and then choose the Crop option.
  3. It is important to note that all pages in the processed documents will be cropped equally.

Frequently Asked Questions about Shipping Label Cropping

Is my PDF file safe and private?

Yes, 100% private. All PDF processing happens directly inside your web browser using client-side libraries. Your shipping label files never leave your device or get uploaded to any server.

How does crop on all pages work?

You draw the crop selection box once on the first page preview, and the exact same margin/crop dimensions are applied automatically to all pages in your bulk shipping label PDF.

Does it support thermal printer label sizes?

Yes, you can custom-resize the crop box to fit 4x6 thermal printer label dimensions or standard A4/A5 shipping label cutouts used by Flipkart, Meesho, and Amazon.
