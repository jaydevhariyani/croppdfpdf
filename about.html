About CropPDFPDF

Built for sellers who need clean shipping labels — fast, free & private.

Who We Are

CropPDFPDF is a free online tool created to solve a real daily problem faced by thousands of e-commerce sellers in India and around the world — cropping shipping labels from platforms like Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Ajio, Amazon, Limeroad, Nykaa and eBay.

We noticed that most PDF crop tools are either complicated, force sign-ups, add watermarks, or process files on external servers. CropPDFPDF is different: everything runs directly in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

Our Mission

To give every seller a simple, beautiful and completely private way to crop multi-page shipping label PDFs in seconds — without technical knowledge or paid software.

What Makes Us Different

  • First page only preview – Only the first page is shown (fast even for 500-page PDFs). One crop applies to every page.
  • Resizable selection box – Draw, move and resize freely (square, long, short or wide) on the first page preview.
  • Zero server upload – 100% client-side processing using modern browser technologies.
  • No account needed – Open the site, upload, crop, download. That’s it.
  • E-commerce focused – Designed specifically with shipping labels from major Indian and global marketplaces in mind.
  • Optional Watermark – Add custom text watermark with control over position, opacity and size for branding or security.

Supported Platforms
