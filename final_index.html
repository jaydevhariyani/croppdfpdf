What is PDF cropping and why it matters for Indian sellers?

PDF cropping changes the visible page area without deleting content permanently. It sets a new page box. For example, a courier company gives you A4 PDF containing 4 shipping labels (2x2 grid). Your thermal printer needs one label per 4x6 roll. By cropping, you select one label quadrant and create a perfect 4x6 print-ready PDF. Similarly, scanned documents often have large black borders — cropping removes them. For e-commerce, correct label size is critical. If you print A4 label on 4x6 paper, barcode gets cut and shipment goes into RTO, costing you money. Our tool lets you crop precisely to 10x15 cm (4x6 inch), the standard size accepted by Delhivery, Xpressbees, Ekart, Blue Dart and DTDC. It supports batch cropping — apply same crop box to all pages.
